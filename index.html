Questions

Straight answers.

Everything people usually ask before installing.

Is Ryvo really free?

Yes, entirely. No paid tier, no ads, no subscription. It's open-source software, built and maintained in the open.

What Android version do I need?

Ryvo runs on Android 7.0 (Nougat) and above.

Does it support lossless audio?

Not yet. Standard streaming quality is supported today, and higher-bitrate playback is on the roadmap.

Can I contribute?

Always. Open an issue, submit a pull request, or suggest a feature on GitHub — all contributors are welcome.
